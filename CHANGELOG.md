# Changelog

## 0.1.5
Placing a wall over an outlet mounted on a powered foundation keeps the connected floors powered.

## 0.1.4
Touching top/bottom faces now conduct power through stacked foundations, including mixed heights and finishes. Gaps and corner-only touches stay isolated. Existing stacks reconnect on load. Updated stacking/reload/vertical-zoop tests.

## 0.1.3
Four red/green corner indicators and five native Customizer finishes. Public mod identity is TrapperPoweredFoundations. Earlier private-ID packages must not be installed as releases of this public mod.

## Earlier development
0.1.2 added direct foundation-side wall outlets. 0.1.1 corrected mesh-height alignment. Historical test records are included for context, not as new release claims.
