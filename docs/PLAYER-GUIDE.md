# Playing with Powered Foundations

1. Buy Powered Foundations from the AWESOME Shop's Foundations category for 25 coupons.
2. Open the build menu and select a powered 1m, 2m or 4m foundation. Ordinary vanilla foundations do not become powered automatically.
3. Build a connected floor. Side contact and directly stacked faces conduct power; air gaps and corner-only touches do not.
4. Place an ordinary power pole on the floor and wire it to your supply. A wall outlet on a foundation side can also feed the floor, as can an outlet on a separate wall whose base touches the floor.
5. Place machines with some of their actual footprint on the powered floor. Watch for green corner indicators and verify power in the machine UI.

Red indicators mean no power or a tripped fuse. Check the generator, feed cable, contact between tiles and available capacity. Removing a connecting tile separates the circuits; replacing it reconnects them. Different feeds on the same connected floor intentionally join their circuits.

Use the normal Customizer for the five supported finishes. Colors/finishes do not remove power or the corner indicators. Existing stacks update automatically when loading 0.1.4; rebuilding is not required.

Hidden floor connections do not consume visible pole cable slots. Ordinary switches are excluded from automatic floor attachment. Generators still supply through normal wiring. Powered walls/ceilings and third-party finishes are outside this release's verified scope.
